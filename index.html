What it does Sign in

Disaster relief coordination

People to a Safer Tomorrow.

ReliefRoute gives the municipal team and barangay representatives one place to record relief requests, mark priority areas, and follow whether goods are still pending or already delivered.

Open the prototype Go to dashboard

Built for Mogpog

Sample records use the public barangay names of Mogpog, Marinduque. The households, counts, and deliveries are fictional.

ReliefRoute does not replace official emergency response, professional disaster assessment, or decisions made by authorized government personnel.

Three working operations

Relief requests

Submit a request with a reference number, barangay, date, assistance type, affected families, priority, and status. Search, filter, edit, and add coordinator remarks.

Priority areas

Track which barangays still need help, what was reported, and which request a sample area is linked to. These labels are coordination notes, not official severity scores.

Delivery and distribution

Create a delivery reference, assign personnel and goods, and update the status from scheduled through delivered, partial, or cancelled.

Who uses it in the demo

Administrator

Dashboard, accounts, all requests, areas, routes, inventory, distribution, and reports.

Relief coordinator

Review requests, set status, organize routes, and monitor distribution. No user-account administration.

Barangay representative

Submit and follow requests for their own barangay, and see related areas and deliveries.

The supporting pages cover a calculated dashboard, sample routes, inventory with low-stock flags, reports with JSON and CSV export, and demo user accounts. Edits stay in this browser through localStorage. The original JSON files are the starting sample, and Export downloads a current copy.
